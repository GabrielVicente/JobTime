JobTime

Know which jobs actually pay.

JobTime is a time log for tradespeople. Say what you worked on, the way you'd tell a friend, and it sorts it into jobs, days and kinds of work. At the end of a job it shows where the hours went, what you billed against your costs, and what the job really paid you per hour.

Help

Logging: type or dictate in the tell tab, or answer the daily reminder straight from the notification. You always see what it found before anything is saved.

Subscription: JobTime Pro unlocks reading what you say. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Your jobs, hours and exports stay available either way.

Your data: Settings › export all data gives you everything as spreadsheets (CSV) and JSON. Settings › delete account erases it all.
