JobTime Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

JobTime helps tradespeople log the hours they spend on each job and see what each job really paid. This page explains what the app collects, where it goes, and what you can do about it. In short: your data is used only to run the app for you, it is never sold, and you can export or delete it at any time.

What JobTime stores

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored hashed; we can't read it).
  • What you enter: jobs (name, client, status, price, quoted hours, start and finish dates, the notes you write about how you refer to a job), hours (date, job, hours, kind of work, note), costs, invoices, and the day recaps you write or dictate.

JobTime does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and it contains no ads or third-party trackers.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, so you can reach it from any of your devices. Each account can only read its own rows. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's Keychain.

AI: how "read it" works

When you tap read it, or answer the daily reminder, JobTime sends the following to Anthropic, whose Claude model sorts your words into jobs, days and hours:

  • what you typed or dictated,
  • your job names and the notes you wrote about how you refer to them,
  • the dates and hours you logged in the last three weeks (so nothing is counted twice).

It does not send your email address, prices, costs or invoices. Anthropic processes this only to return the result. Its commercial terms don't allow it to train its models on this data. Anthropic keeps requests for up to 30 days and then deletes them; a request its automated safety systems flag for review may be kept for up to 2 years. JobTime asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings. Without it, you can still log hours by hand.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. JobTime receives confirmation that a subscription is active, never your payment details.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › export all data gives you every job, hour, cost, invoice and recap as spreadsheets (CSV) and JSON.
  • Delete: Settings › delete account erases your account and all of its data permanently. Cancel any subscription separately in your Apple Account settings.
  • Correct: everything you enter can be edited or deleted in the app.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data and don't share it with anyone except the providers above, who process it only to run JobTime, or where the law requires it.

Children

JobTime is a work tool and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
